## 32.1 Suggested Combinations

- **Beginner web:** PortSwigger Academy → Juice Shop → OWASP guides.
- **Networking:** Cisco Networking Academy → Wireshark labs → Nmap in your lab.
- **Cloud:** AWS Skill Builder / Microsoft Learn → Prowler in a free-tier account.
- **Reverse/binary:** OpenSecurityTraining2 → OverTheWire Narnia → small fuzzing projects.
- **AI security:** OWASP GenAI resources → Promptfoo/Garak labs → local model experiments.

## 32.2 Evaluating a Course

Ask:

1. Does it include hands-on labs, or only video lectures?
2. Is the content current (version-sensitive topics)?
3. Are learning objectives and prerequisites stated?
4. Is there a community or instructor support?
5. Does it align with your roadmap month?

## 32.3 Key Takeaways

- Combine structured courses with deliberate lab practice; courses alone are insufficient.
- Many excellent resources are free; paid certificates are optional signals.
- Re-evaluate your course choices each quarter against your roadmap.
