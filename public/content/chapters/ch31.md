## 31.9 How to Build a Reference Library

1. Prioritize one book per foundational area before specializing.
2. Keep notes with page references and your own examples.
3. Cross-check book content with current standards and official docs (books age).
4. Use legal access: purchase, library, or free official editions. Do not use pirated copies.

## 31.10 Key Takeaways

- Choose books by level and prerequisite; foundations first.
- Use official publisher or legal/free sources.
- Treat fast-moving areas (cloud, AI) with current docs alongside books.
